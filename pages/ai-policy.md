title: Use of AI
order: 25
---

You may use AI tools when you contribute to ACT Rules, as long as you take full responsibility for what you post. This policy applies to issues, comments, pull requests, reviews and anything else in the ACT Rules repositories.

## Disclose it

If AI wrote or substantially shaped your contribution, say so. One line is enough, for example: "Drafted with an AI assistant, reviewed and edited by me."

You don't need to disclose spell checking, grammar fixes, or translation of your own words.

## Review it

Before you post AI-generated content, make sure that you:

- have read all of it,
- understand it, and can explain it if asked,
- agree with it, and
- checked that its facts and references are correct, including links, WCAG references, and HTML or ARIA behavior.

You are responsible for what you post, whether or not AI helped write it.

## Keep it short

Other people's time is what limits this group. AI tools tend to be verbose, so edit their output down before you post it:

- Say what you want to say once, and leave out filler and generic background.
- Don't restate the issue, the rule, or earlier comments.
- In reviews, comment on what needs to change. Don't summarize the changes back to the author.

## Rules, definitions and examples

Rules, definitions and examples written with AI need human verification, just like any other content:

- In the pull request, say which parts were written with AI.
- Check every example by hand, including that it passes, fails or is inapplicable as stated and that it behaves that way in browsers and assistive technologies.
- The usual [review process](../design/process/) and [Definition of "Done"](../design/definition-of-done) apply.

## If a contribution doesn't follow this policy

Facilitators may ask you to disclose AI use, or to shorten or rework a contribution. Contributions that are not updated may be closed.

This policy is about how contributors use AI. How AI-based tools are listed as implementations is a separate discussion ([issue #2473](https://github.com/act-rules/act-rules.github.io/issues/2473)).
